YOUR FIRST WEEK

From settings to teaching.

  1. Choose a start date and customise your teaching day.
  2. Explore the previews. Click page tabs and dates to try the navigation.
  3. Download your PDF and import it into a PDF annotation app.
  4. Use your app’s navigation or reading mode to follow links, and its pen mode to write.

This generator creates blank planning pages. Handwriting and typing happen in your PDF app. The monthly calendar links only the seven dates included in your planner. App support varies; test a download on your device.
